Privacy Notice

Your privacy matters

This notice explains how The Hope & Healing Hub collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit this website, get in touch, or use our services. Last updated September 2026.

Who we are

The Hope & Healing Hub provides Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), mental health information and psychological wellbeing resources.

For data protection purposes, The Hope & Healing Hub is responsible for the personal information we collect and use.

If you have any questions about your privacy or how your information is handled, you can contact us at hello@hopeandhealinghub.com.

What information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with The Hope & Healing Hub, we may collect information including:

  • Your name and contact details
  • Information you provide through our contact or enquiry forms
  • Appointment and booking information
  • Information you choose to share when accessing therapy, including information about your health and personal circumstances
  • Brief clinical records where required for safe and professional practice
  • Payment and transaction information
  • Information about how you use our website, where analytics or cookies are used

We only aim to collect information that is necessary for providing and operating our services.

How we use your information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries
  • Arrange and provide therapy services
  • Manage appointments
  • Process payments
  • Maintain appropriate clinical and administrative records
  • Meet legal, safeguarding and professional obligations
  • Improve our website and services
  • Send resources or updates where you have chosen to receive them

We do not sell your personal information.

Our legal basis for using your information

Under UK data protection law, we need a lawful reason for collecting and using personal information. Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Contract, where information is needed to arrange or provide a service you have requested
  • Legitimate interests, where information is reasonably necessary to operate The Hope & Healing Hub safely and effectively
  • Legal obligation, where we are required to use or retain information by law
  • Consent, where you have specifically chosen to receive certain communications or allow particular uses of your information

Health information receives additional protection under data protection law. Where we process this type of information, we will ensure that an appropriate lawful basis and special-category condition applies.

Who we may share information with

Your information is treated confidentially. It may be processed by trusted services that help us operate The Hope & Healing Hub, such as our website, email, booking, payment or clinical record systems.

Information may also need to be shared where:

  • There is a serious concern about your safety or somebody else's safety
  • There is a safeguarding concern
  • We are legally required to disclose information
  • Information is appropriately discussed within professional clinical supervision

Where possible and appropriate, we would discuss a disclosure with you first. A more detailed explanation of confidentiality will be provided before therapy begins.

How we keep your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information and use appropriate systems and security measures to prevent unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

No method of sending or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid including highly sensitive or urgent information in the website contact form.

How long we keep your information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, and to meet relevant legal, insurance and professional requirements. Different types of information may therefore be kept for different lengths of time. More detailed retention information will be provided to therapy clients where appropriate.

Cookies

This website currently uses only the technical cookies needed to make the contact and waitlist forms work securely. We don't currently use analytics or tracking cookies. If that changes in the future, we'll ask for your consent first, in line with UK cookie rules, before any non-essential cookies are used.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights over your personal information. These can include the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete information in certain circumstances
  • Ask us to restrict how information is used
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Receive certain information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent

Some of these rights may be limited where we have a legal or professional obligation to retain information. To ask a question or exercise your rights, contact hello@hopeandhealinghub.com.

Making a complaint

If you have concerns about how your personal information has been handled, please contact The Hope & Healing Hub first so that we can look into your concerns. You also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator.

Changes to this notice

We may update this privacy notice as The Hope & Healing Hub develops, our services change, or data protection requirements are updated. The latest version will always be available on this website.
