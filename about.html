Meet Hope

Hi, I'm Hope

Founder of The Hope & Healing Hub · Cognitive Behavioural Therapist

I'm Hope, a Cognitive Behavioural Therapist and founder of The Hope & Healing Hub. I help people make sense of the patterns that can leave them feeling stuck, and work with them to find practical ways forward.

In the room together

Therapy, without the therapy-speak

I'm not here to sit silently, nod occasionally, and leave you wondering what you're supposed to do next. My approach to CBT is collaborative, practical and curious: we make sense of what's happening together, look at what's keeping things going, and experiment with ways of doing things differently.

Collaboration

We'll work things out together, not you being told what to do.

Curiosity

We'll get interested in the patterns, rather than judging them.

Practicality

Something you can actually use outside the room, not just in it.

Your pace

Progress doesn't have to look perfect to count.

The name

Why The Hope & Healing Hub?

The name is more than a name. It represents the kind of mental health space I want to create.

Hope

Things can change, even when they don't feel like they can right now.

Healing

Progress doesn't have to be linear to count.

Hub

Therapy is only one part of the picture.

The Hub is being built as more than a therapy practice: a space where psychological therapy, accessible mental health education and practical resources can live together, and grow into something bigger over time.

"Therapy isn't about changing who you are."

It's about understanding yourself better, recognising patterns that aren't helping anymore, and developing different ways of responding.

A little more human

Therapist, and also a person

I started out in lower-intensity CBT work, and became increasingly interested in not just what someone was experiencing, but what was keeping it going. That curiosity is what pulled me further into CBT, and eventually into work with more complex presentations.

Outside of work, I'm creative: I make content, travel when I can, and I'm a bit of a foodie who's always looking for somewhere new to try. Health and wellbeing matter to me too.

Having a life outside therapy reminds me of something important: we're all whole people, not a diagnosis.

We're all more than our difficult days, and I try to bring that same perspective into the therapy room.
