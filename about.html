Meet Hope

Hi, I'm Hope

Founder of The Hope & Healing Hub · Cognitive Behavioural Therapist

I'm a Cognitive Behavioural Therapist with a passion for helping people better understand themselves, their thoughts, emotions and behaviours — and supporting them to make meaningful, sustainable changes in their lives.

I created The Hope & Healing Hub from a simple belief: mental health support should feel human, accessible and relevant to the person sitting in front of you.

Therapy isn't about having all the answers. It's about creating a space where you can pause, understand what is happening, and begin to work through things at your own pace.

My approach

My work is grounded in Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), an evidence-based psychological therapy that explores the relationship between our thoughts, feelings, physical sensations and behaviours.

I believe therapy should be collaborative. Rather than simply telling you what to do, I work alongside you to understand what may be keeping difficulties going, identify patterns, and develop practical strategies that can help you move towards the life you want.

For me, effective therapy is both evidence-based and person-centred.

Your experiences, identity, relationships, culture and circumstances all matter. There isn't a one-size-fits-all approach to mental health, so therapy should be adapted to the individual rather than expecting the individual to fit the therapy.

The name

Why The Hope & Healing Hub?

The name is more than a name — it represents the kind of mental health space I want to create.

Hope

The possibility that things can change, even when they don't feel like they can right now.

Healing

Recognising that progress isn't always linear — it involves understanding, processing, rebuilding confidence and learning new ways of responding.

Hub

Connection — a place where psychological therapy, mental health education, practical resources and conversations about wellbeing come together.

My vision is to create a space where people can access psychological support and information that feels welcoming, relatable and grounded in evidence.

Areas of interest

My clinical interests include supporting people experiencing difficulties such as:

Anxiety & worry Low mood & depression Low self-esteem Overthinking & rumination Stress & emotional difficulties Long-term health conditions Psychosis & related experiences

I recognise that mental health difficulties can look very different from person to person. My aim is to understand your individual experience and work collaboratively with you to identify what would be most helpful.

A space for you

Whether you're experiencing anxiety, low mood, difficulties with self-esteem, overthinking, stress, challenges associated with a long-term health condition, or other difficulties with your mental health, you don't have to navigate everything alone.

Therapy can provide an opportunity to understand what you're experiencing, develop new skills, and begin making changes that feel meaningful to you.

I aim to create a therapeutic environment where you can feel heard without judgement, supported without pressure, and empowered to take an active role in your own journey.

There is no expectation that you arrive knowing exactly what you need. Sometimes, the first step is simply being able to say:

"I'm struggling, and I want things to feel different."

That's enough of a starting point.

My philosophy

I don't believe therapy is about changing who you are.

It's about developing a better understanding of yourself, recognising patterns that may no longer be serving you, and building the confidence and skills to respond differently.

Change can take time. Progress can be messy. And there may be moments when moving forward feels difficult. But small steps can still matter.

You deserve a space where your experiences are taken seriously, your voice is heard, and your goals are at the centre of the work.
