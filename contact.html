Before you get in touch

A few things you might be wondering

Do I need a diagnosis to contact you?

No. You don't need a diagnosis or even a clear idea of what's going on. We can start by making sense of what you've been experiencing and what you'd like to change.

What if I'm not sure CBT is right for me?

That's okay. Getting in touch doesn't commit you to anything. Part of our first conversation is working out together whether CBT feels like a suitable fit for what you're looking for.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes, enquiries are treated confidentially. There are limited circumstances where confidentiality has legal limits (for example, a serious risk of harm), and I'll always explain these clearly before we begin working together.

How quickly will you reply?

Usually within 2 working days. Messages sent outside working hours will be picked up when I'm next available.

How much do sessions cost?

Pricing isn't confirmed yet, as I'm not taking new clients just yet. I'll share this clearly on the site as soon as it's decided, well before anything is booked.

One more thing

This site, and the form above, isn't set up for emergencies, so if you or someone you know needs help right now, please don't wait on a reply from me.

Call 999 or go to your nearest A&E if there's immediate risk to life. Samaritans are free, any time, day or night, on 116 123. Or text SHOUT to 85258 for free, confidential crisis support by text.
