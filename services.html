Therapy at The Hope & Healing Hub

Therapy that makes sense in real life

I offer 1:1 cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT), online. It isn't about thinking positively or pretending difficult things aren't difficult. It's about understanding what's happening, spotting the patterns that keep you stuck, and finding practical ways to respond differently.

What we treat

Support for what's actually going on

Every therapy plan is tailored, but these are some of the areas where CBT can be helpful.

Anxiety & worry

Your mind won't switch off.

Racing thoughts and constant "what ifs" can leave you stuck, even avoiding things just to escape them. CBT helps you interrupt the cycle and respond differently.

Depression & low mood

Everything feels harder than it used to.

Low energy and pulling away from things you enjoy tend to build on each other. CBT helps you rebuild momentum, one small step at a time.

Stress & burnout

You're doing a lot, but it never feels like enough.

Constantly switched on with no real recovery makes it hard to know where to start. We'll find what's driving it, then build habits that actually hold.

Trauma

Something happened, and it still feels present.

Difficult experiences can keep shaping how you think and react long after they're over. Therapy creates space to process it at your own pace.

Self-esteem

That inner critic has a lot to say.

Constant comparison and never feeling "enough" often trace back to old beliefs. CBT helps you test those beliefs against the evidence.

OCD

The thought keeps coming back, and you keep responding to make it go away.

Checking, reassurance-seeking and compulsions can take over more of your day than you'd like. We'll work on breaking that cycle together.

How we work

What does CBT actually look like?

We explore what's happening, connect the dots, try practical CBT strategies and reflect on what works.

01

Notice

What's happening? Thoughts, emotions, behaviours, physical sensations and the situation around them.

02

Understand

We connect the dots and build a shared understanding of what's keeping the difficulty going.

03

Experiment

This is where therapy leaves the room. We test things, practise skills, and try different responses in real life.

04

Reflect

What changed? What did you learn? What do we keep, adapt, or try next?

Therapy is collaborative and tailored to you. I bring the CBT knowledge; you bring the understanding of your own life. Together we decide what to focus on, and we go at a pace that works for you.
